## RouteHopMarkers.tsx

**Purpose:** Shared Routes-panel hop breadcrumb markers for route plans and the Current Route panel.

### RouteHopBreadcrumb

### HopSquare
Security-coloured square/circle hop marker with tooltip (name, tag, via).
